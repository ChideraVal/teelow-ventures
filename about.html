About Us

Teelow Printing World is a trusted printing and promotional products business based in Mushin, Lagos, focused on helping individuals, businesses, organizations, and event planners bring their ideas to life. We specialize in the buying and selling of quality awards, souvenirs, mugs, flasks, keyholders, heat press machines, machine components, tags, and other printing and personalization products.

Whether you need an award for recognition, customized souvenirs for an event, branded mugs and flasks, keyholders, or the equipment and components needed for printing, we provide a wide range of products to meet different needs and budgets. Our goal is to make it easier for customers to find the right products for their personal, corporate, promotional, and event needs.

At Teelow Printing World, we believe that every idea deserves to be brought to life with quality and attention to detail. From simple personalized items to bulk orders and business branding needs, we are committed to providing reliable products and helpful service. Get your imagination printed.
