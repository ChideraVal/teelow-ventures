Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions businesses usually ask before booking a demo.

What file types does Zendix support?

Zendix supports PDFs, PNG, JPEG/JPG, WEBP, TXT files, and email attachments.

Can teams review extracted data before using it?

Yes. Teams can compare extracted data with the source file, edit values, and approve results.

Can Zendix handle different departments or customers?

Yes. Multi-environment workflows let businesses separate documents by department, client, or workflow.

Does Zendix support email automation?

Yes. It can automatically process incoming business emails and attachments on a schedule or manually.

Can extracted data be exported?

Yes. Clean structured data can be exported to CSV or Excel for use in other tools.

How does Zendix reduce errors?

Zendix includes review workflows and fail reason detection so teams can fix issues before export.
