ABOUT OUR BUSINESS

Teelow Printing World Ventures

Get your imagination printed. We specialize in awards, souvenirs, and personalized products for individuals, businesses, and special occasions.

Teelow Printing World Ventures is a printing and promotional products business based in Mushin, Lagos, Nigeria. We help individuals, businesses, and organizations find suitable awards, souvenirs, and personalized items for celebrations, corporate events, recognition ceremonies, and promotional activities.

Our product selection includes crystal awards, wooden awards, acrylic awards, gold figurine awards, keyholders, name tags, and other souvenir items. Whether you need awards to recognize achievements or customized souvenirs for an event, we offer products to help you create memorable impressions.

We also buy and sell printing-related products and equipment, providing customers with options for personalized gifts, promotional materials, and recognition items.
